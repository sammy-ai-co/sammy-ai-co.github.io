Research Interests

I am interested in robot manipulation and autonomy, especially Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models that let robot arms and humanoid robots perform real-world tasks from camera input and language instructions.

At Beyond Robotics, I already work hands-on with this stack: I integrate vision models (RF-DETR) into ROS 2 pipelines and work on Behavior Tree control for robot arms: I set up the camera and built the Behavior Tree for a pick-and-place cell on my own, and modified the control of a dual-arm strawberry harvesting robot. The next step I want to study is replacing these hand-designed behaviors with learned VLA policies.

Before robotics, I spent four years in validation at John Deere (HIL circuit-fault regression testing) and Hyundai (CAN-log and signal analysis). I bring that discipline to research: testing systematically before running on real hardware, and debugging robot systems end to end.
